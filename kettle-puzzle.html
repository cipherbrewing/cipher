A little thank you from us.

When we started Cipher, we had a lot of ideas, a lot of things we wanted to build and no real way of knowing where it would all lead.

Today, seeing something we make in our factory find its way to your brew bar is a feeling we still find hard to put into words.

Every order, every message and every time you recommend us to another coffee person, it means more than you probably realise. We know there are plenty of choices out there. The fact that you chose us and trusted us to be a part of your coffee ritual is something we will never take for granted.

This little puzzle was our way of adding something extra to your box. A small reminder that behind every product, there's a team putting in the hours, figuring things out and caring a little too much about getting things right.

Thank you for letting the things we make become a part of your world. From our factory to your brew bar, we're grateful for every single one of you who has made this journey possible.

We're just getting started.
